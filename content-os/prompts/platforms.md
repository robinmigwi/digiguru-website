# Platform Adaptation Rules

## Instagram
Open with the idea visually. Keep spoken language compact. Use strong on screen text and polished motion. The caption should deepen the point rather than repeat the video.

## TikTok
Prioritize pattern interruption, direct language and a fast payoff. The first sentence should create a reason to keep watching. Avoid sounding like an advertisement.

## LinkedIn
Lead with an observation, business story, disagreement or lesson. Prefer founder voice and evidence. Explain the commercial implication. Keep the post native to LinkedIn instead of reposting an Instagram caption.

## Facebook
Start from a familiar business situation. Make the story understandable without specialist knowledge. Favor practical explanations and shareable scenarios.

## Universal rule
One master idea can power all four platforms, but the hook, pacing, caption and CTA must be adapted for the platform. Never cross post the same copy unchanged.
