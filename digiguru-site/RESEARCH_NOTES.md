# DigiGuru Website Research Notes

## Conversational / lead conversion companies studied

1. Intercom — strong outcome framing, "chatbots answer, AI agents resolve", visible customer journey, action-oriented conversations, human handoff and integrations.
2. Podium — direct commercial promise, lead coverage, response speed, booking and revenue outcomes, clear audience for local businesses.
3. Respond.io — revenue-critical conversations, cross-channel journey, lead qualification, CRM updates, product recommendation, bookings and human-in-the-loop.
4. SleekFlow — revenue-driving conversations, full customer journey, AI workforce, web visitor capture, catalogues, payments and CRM connections.
5. Smith.ai — end-to-end intake framing: answer, qualify, book, convert; strong visual explanation of operational workflow.
6. Manychat — strong clarity around social + WhatsApp automation, moving from engagement to qualified leads and sales.
7. Wati — clear anti-friction messaging around no forms, no delays, no lost leads; capture everywhere then qualify and route.
8. HighLevel — "stop duct-taping tools" positioning, unified inbox, CRM, conversations and workflow automation in one system.
9. Qualified — problem first: slow lead response and form-based capture; real-time conversational engagement tied directly to pipeline.
10. Drift / Salesloft — "intent-to-action gap" framing, buyer signals, personalized conversations and seller actions.

## Prompt / frontend build research studied

1. OpenAI frontend prompt instructions — audience empathy, domain-specific UI, explicit taste and quality guidance.
2. OpenAI prompt engineering — concrete instructions, coding-oriented structure, recommended frontend stack and stronger zero-to-one prompts.
3. Anthropic prompt engineering / frontend aesthetics — distinctive typography, cohesive color systems, avoiding generic AI aesthetics, asymmetry, motion and spatial composition.
4. Vercel v0 prompting guide — define context of use, constraints and taste; tell the model who uses the site, when, what decisions they make and how it should feel.
5. Cursor Rules — persistent project instructions, AGENTS.md, version controlled design/coding rules, concrete and scoped guidance.
6. Bolt prompting guide — specific prompts beat vague ones; keep one workflow coherent per prompt; name exact screens/elements and what must not change.
7. Replit AI Foundations — strong prompts specify goal, context, URLs, assets and design; describe, critique and iterate; mobile and visual validation matter.
8. Lovable Prompting Bible — structured prompts with context, task, guidelines and constraints; put important information early; use specificity.
9. Lovable frontend development guidance — mobile-first, performance, accessibility, React/Vite/Tailwind and frontend as interaction experience rather than decoration.
10. Google Stitch — prompt + image/reference driven UI design, designer/developer collaboration and turning design intent into frontend code.

## Resulting design principles

The DigiGuru site prompt deliberately requires:

- story before services
- problem recognition before solution explanation
- visual before/after contrast
- product-like system visualization
- real conversation examples
- explicit business outcomes
- configurable CTA infrastructure
- a distinctive visual identity
- mobile-first implementation
- performance and accessibility
- persistent AI project rules
- test-and-fix workflow before completion
