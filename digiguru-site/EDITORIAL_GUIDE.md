# DigiGuru Editorial Guide

## Editorial promise
Help business owners understand what happens between customer attention and a real business outcome. Every piece should explain a decision, show the process behind it, and leave the reader with a useful action. Write for owners and teams in Kenya first, while making the advice useful beyond Kenya.

## Editorial standards
- Publish genuinely useful, original advice with clear examples and context.
- Do not invent statistics, conversion lifts, testimonials, client approvals or results.
- Distinguish a request received from a booking confirmed, and an enquiry from a sale.
- Use direct headings, clear definitions, descriptive titles, helpful page links and accurate metadata.
- Link to authoritative sources where external guidance informs a claim.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, fake local pages and claims that AI search has secret ranking hacks.
- Keep the DigiGuru positioning centred on a connected customer journey, not a list of disconnected services or chatbot features.
- Review every CTA, form, mobile layout, source link and factual claim before publication.

## Measurement
Set up Google Search Console and analytics only after confirming the correct property, consent and privacy approach. Establish the launch baseline for impressions, clicks, queries and index coverage. Separately measure useful commercial actions such as enquiry starts, form submissions, booking requests and completed sales where the business can accurately track them. Do not present traffic or rankings as revenue.
