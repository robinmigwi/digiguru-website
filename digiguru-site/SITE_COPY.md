# DigiGuru website core copy

## Positioning

**Your marketing gets attention. We help turn that interest into sales.**

DigiGuru connects how people discover a business with what happens when they ask a question, need advice, want to book or are ready to buy. One clearer path from first click to the next business action.

## The journey

**Attract → Converse → Convert → Continue**

- Attract: sponsored ads, content, SEO, AEO, websites and landing pages help the right people discover the business.
- Converse: a business specific WhatsApp Concierge uses configured business information to answer questions and guide people.
- Convert: the conversation can lead to an order, booking, quote request, qualification step or handoff.
- Continue: connected records, follow up, reminders, reporting and improvement help the team carry the context forward.

## Lavington Green Dental Suite

DigiGuru built the website and a functioning appointment booking flow for Lavington Green Dental Suite. The project includes responsive website pages for dental services, the practice, team and facilities; structured patient information and review content; and an appointment Concierge flow that captures what a patient needs and carries those details into the clinic's booking process.
