# DigiGuru Company Brief
## Conversational sales systems that turn attention into action

### 1. What DigiGuru is

DigiGuru is a **conversational sales systems company**.

We help businesses connect the journey between marketing attention, customer conversation and business action.

A business can attract someone through a Meta ad, social content, Google search, a website, a referral or an existing customer relationship. The customer then needs answers, confidence and a clear next step.

DigiGuru builds the layer that carries that customer forward.

**Attract → Converse → Understand → Act → Continue**

The company is not positioned as a conventional digital marketing agency and not as a chatbot vendor. Sponsored advertising, websites, WhatsApp systems, CRM connections and automation are components of a larger commercial system.

---

### 2. The business problem

Many businesses are good at creating attention but lose momentum after the lead arrives.

The pattern is familiar:

A customer sees an ad.

They click.

They visit the website or open WhatsApp.

They ask a question.

They wait.

They repeat the question.

The next step is unclear.

Someone follows up later, or the lead disappears.

The result is a gap between **lead generation and conversion**.

DigiGuru exists to close that gap.

The Concierge is shaped around the individual business.

FAQs

Policies
Availability

This allows the conversation to be useful without pretending that every business operates the same way.

The Concierge can:

### 5. Sponsored growth

DigiGuru does not ignore the top of the funnel.

When a business needs more demand, we can run sponsored campaigns designed around the conversion journey.

The main acquisition focus is:

**Meta → WhatsApp → Conversation → Action**

Instead of treating paid advertising as a standalone service, the campaign is designed with the destination conversation in mind.

This means the business can understand:

Where the lead came from
What the lead is asking
How the lead is being qualified
What the next step is
What happens after the conversation

The objective is not simply more clicks.

**The objective is more useful customer conversations and more movement toward business outcomes.**

### 7. How DigiGuru works

#### 01 — Map the business

Understand the offer, customers, sales process, questions, availability, data, tools and current points of friction.

#### 02 — Map the conversation

Design what should happen after the customer says, “I'm interested.”

#### 03 — Build the system

Connect acquisition, entry points, business knowledge, Concierge behaviour and business actions.

#### 04 — Test real scenarios

Use real customer questions, objections, edge cases and handoffs.

#### 05 — Launch and improve

Look at where customers hesitate, what they ask and where the journey can become simpler.
