# DigiGuru Frontend Rules

DigiGuru is a conversational sales systems company, not a generic digital agency and not simply a chatbot company.

## Language
Use plain commercial language. Prefer: conversational sales system, Concierge, customer journey, lead conversion, connected systems, booking, order, enquiry, follow up, human handoff.

Avoid inflated AI jargon and unsupported claims.

## Design
Use DigiGuru dark green, gold and teal with warm editorial neutrals. Avoid purple AI gradients, generic SaaS dashboards, repeated card grids, stock illustrations and decorative slop.

Prefer visual storytelling, meaningful flow diagrams, strong typography, asymmetry, whitespace and subtle motion.

## Truthfulness
Never invent customer statistics, testimonials, logos, results or quotes.

## CTA
The WhatsApp number must be configured in one place only. Do not hardcode it throughout the project.

## Accessibility
Maintain semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus, good contrast, reduced motion and usable mobile interactions.

## Performance
Prefer CSS/SVG for visuals. Avoid unnecessary dependencies, autoplay video and heavy effects that slow the first experience.
