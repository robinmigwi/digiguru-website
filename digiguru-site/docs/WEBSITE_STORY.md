# DigiGuru Website Story

1. Attention: businesses already spend effort generating attention.
2. The gap: the lead often loses momentum between click, enquiry and response.
3. The shift: make the conversation part of the sale.
4. Acquisition: DigiGuru can create sponsored demand, especially through Meta and WhatsApp focused campaigns.
5. Concierge: a business specific conversational layer understands offers, services, FAQs, policies, availability and context.
6. Connected action: the conversation can move toward a booking, order, enquiry, quote, CRM update, calendar step, follow up or human handoff.
7. Impact: less waiting, less repetition, clearer action and better handoffs.
8. Proof: real journeys including Noka Foods, Eish Accessories and Lavington Green Dental.
9. CTA: start a conversation on WhatsApp.
