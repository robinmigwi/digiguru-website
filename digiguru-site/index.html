What businesses in Nairobi and across Kenya usually ask about WhatsApp sales systems, SEO, AEO and getting started.

What is a conversational sales system?

A conversational sales system connects how customers find your business with what happens when they ask a question, need advice or want to book. DigiGuru combines search and social marketing, a website, a WhatsApp Concierge and follow up tools, so interest moves towards a booking, order or sale.

What does DigiGuru do for businesses in Nairobi and across Kenya?

DigiGuru is a digital growth partner based in Nairobi. We build the customer journey from discovery to action: SEO and answer engine optimisation, Meta and WhatsApp focused advertising, websites and landing pages, a business specific WhatsApp Concierge, booking flows and follow up. We work with businesses across Kenya.

Can WhatsApp automate bookings and enquiries for my business in Kenya?

Yes, for the common steps. A WhatsApp Concierge can answer questions using your business information, collect the service and preferred time, qualify the enquiry and pass the details to your team or calendar. A person can take over whenever confirmation or judgement is needed.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps people find your business through search engines such as Google. AEO, or answer engine optimisation, helps your business information appear clearly in answer led and AI powered search, including AI overviews and chat assistants. Both belong at the discovery stage, and both depend on clear and accurate business information.

Do you offer local SEO in Nairobi?

Yes. Local SEO covers how your business appears for searches near you, including your Google Business Profile, accurate contact details, service and location pages, and reviews from real customers. We tailor it to the areas you serve, whether that is one Nairobi neighbourhood or several towns.

Why is my business not showing on Google, and can you help?

Common causes are an unclaimed or incomplete Google Business Profile, a website that is slow or hard for search engines to read, thin service pages and unclear signals about where you operate. We start by looking at where customers drop out of the journey, then fix the discovery gaps first.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your business needs, so we do not publish a one size fits all price. After a short conversation about your customers and where they get stuck, we recommend a practical starting point and give you a clear quote.
